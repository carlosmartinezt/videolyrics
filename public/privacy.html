Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 August 2026

videolyrics.org turns an audio file and pasted lyrics into a lyric video. This page describes exactly what leaves your computer, what reaches ours, who else sees it, and how long any of it lasts.

The short version. Your audio and your lyrics are sent to our server so the words can be timed to the vocal. The audio is deleted six hours later. Your reference pictures never leave your browser. The finished video is made on your own machine and is never uploaded to us.

What we receive, and why

What never reaches us

  • Your reference pictures. They are read inside your browser; only the colours pulled out of them are sent.
  • The finished video. Every frame is drawn and encoded on your own machine. The MP4 is saved directly by you and is never uploaded.
  • Your password. Sign-in is by emailed link or by Google. We never see or store a password.

Cookies

Two kinds. A sign-in cookie, set only when you sign in, which is required for the service to work. And Google Analytics cookies, which are not set unless you accept them. Declining analytics does not limit anything.

Rate limits and abuse

Your IP address is used in memory to apply rate limits, so one visitor cannot exhaust a shared server. It is not stored alongside your account.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and everything attached to it. Write to carlosmartinezt@gmail.com and we will act within 30 days.

Because uploads are deleted after six hours, a request about a specific song is usually moot by the time it arrives — there is nothing left to delete.

Children

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it.
