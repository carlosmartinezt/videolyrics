Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

videolyrics.org is a page about a lyric video tool that is not open yet. The only thing it asks for is an email address, so we can tell you when it opens. This page says what happens to it.

The short version. If you leave your email, it is sent to the person who runs the site, once, and used for one thing: telling you when videolyrics opens. It is never sold or shared. Nothing else is asked for.

Cookies

The site sets none of its own. Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits, and YouTube may set them if you play a video. Blocking them in your browser does not stop the page or the form from working.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, including taking your email off the list. Write to carlosmartinezt@gmail.com and we will act within 30 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it. When videolyrics opens, this page will be rewritten to cover the tool itself.
