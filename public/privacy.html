Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

videolyrics.org turns an audio file and pasted lyrics into a lyric video. This page describes exactly what leaves your computer, what reaches ours, who else sees it, and how long any of it lasts.

The short version. It is free and there is no account. Your audio and your lyrics are sent to our server so the words can be timed to the vocal. The audio is deleted six hours later. Your reference pictures never leave your browser. The finished video is made on your own machine and is never uploaded to us.

What we receive, and why

WhatWhyHow long we keep it
Your audio fileTo detect when each word is sung, and to read tempo, key and loudnessDeleted after 6 hours
Your lyricsTo align the words to the vocal and to choose a design that suits themDeleted with the job, after 6 hours
Colours from your reference picturesTo steer the palette. Only a short list of colour values is sent, never the picturesDeleted with the job
Title and artist, if you enter themTo draw the title cardDeleted with the job
The song's file name, its first line of lyrics, and your countryEmailed to the person who runs the site when a video is started or downloaded, so he knows it is being used. Never your IP addressAs long as that email is kept
Usage measurementsTo see whether anyone finishes a video. Counts and durations only, never lyrics, titles, file names or email addressesAs held by Google Analytics

What never reaches us

  • Your reference pictures. They are read inside your browser; only the colours pulled out of them are sent.
  • The finished video. Every frame is drawn and encoded on your own machine. The MP4 is saved directly by you and is never uploaded.
  • Who you are. There is no sign-up and no account, so we never ask for your name, email address or a password.

Who else processes your data

CloudflareServes the site and sees the usual request metadata, including your IP address.

Cookies

Only Google Analytics cookies, and they are not set unless you accept them. Declining analytics does not limit anything.

Rate limits and abuse

Your IP address is used in memory to apply rate limits, so one visitor cannot exhaust a shared server. It is not written to disk.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to carlosmartinezt@gmail.com and we will act within 30 days.

Because uploads are deleted after six hours, a request about a specific song is usually moot by the time it arrives, because there is nothing left to delete.

Children

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it.
